How We Teach Our Photography Classes

Camera Basics

We know all those buttons and dials can be overwhelming. We will walk you through the “how, what and why” of your cameras settings in a fun, user-friendly environment. Our classes are space limited allowing for individual instruction.

Exercises

Most of us learn by doing! That is why we take the time to step away from the classroom and go outside. We practice how to get that blurry background, how to freeze action like wine droplets or someone jumping in the air.

Put it all together

We bring it all together and talk through equipment, composition, lighting and camera artistry. Our students walk away with knowledge and confidence to use what they’ve learned to capture their stories. Come join us!

Our Classes

Private Lessons

Our private lessons are focused on Basic Photography, but if you want to dive a little deeper Christiana is up for that too! The class can focus on Basics, Advanced or specific areas such as Portrait Photography (which is her specialty!) Private lessons are $150 and hour with a minimum of 2 hours.

Group Basic Photography Class

Our Basic Photography class is a fun way to learn camera essentials while enjoying Washington wines with friends. We host groups up to 8 and can tailor the lesson to your interests. Contact Christiana for a quote and to schedule your session.

Group iPhone Photography Class

Looking for a fun night out that’s not just another game night—or a team event that beats the usual happy hour? Try the iPhone Photography Class! Grab your crew and join Christiana to learn how to take amazing iPhone photos while sipping great Washington wine.
